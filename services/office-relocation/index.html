Office Relocation Services

Moving offices has a lot of moving parts. The new space, the lease, the layout, the furniture and the timing. Easy Spaces helps with all of it, so you have one team to call.

What we help with

  • Finding the space. Work with a licensed tenant rep. Tenant matching is free for you. See Tenant Rep Partners.
  • Reviewing the lease. Free, before you sign.
  • Planning the layout. A 3D layout of the new office. See Space Planning & 3D.
  • Furnishing it. Buy, subscribe or rent-to-own.

Timing

Furniture lead time is usually 2 to 4 weeks after you approve the plan. Start the conversation early so the furniture is ready when your lease starts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an office relocation take on the furniture side?

Usually 2 to 4 weeks after you approve the plan.

Can I furnish the new office with no upfront payment?

Yes. Rent-to-own is $349/mo with $0 upfront, and you own the furniture at the end. Delivery and install are quoted separately. A subscription starts from $379/mo with $0 upfront and a 12-month minimum. Delivery and install are built into the monthly price, with free removal at the end of the term.

Do you help find the new space?

Yes. You work with a licensed tenant rep, and matching is free for tenants.

Will you review my new lease?

Yes. Lease review is free.
