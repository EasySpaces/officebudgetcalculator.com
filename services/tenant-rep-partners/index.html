Tenant Rep Partners

Looking for office space? Get someone on your side of the table. Jason Bowman, President of Easy Spaces, is a licensed agent, and we work with tenant rep partners to match you with the right space.

Matching is free for the tenant. So is a lease review. If you already have a lease in hand, send it over and we will walk you through what to watch for before you sign.

What you get

  • Help finding spaces that fit your headcount, location and budget.
  • A free review of the lease before you sign.
  • Help asking for concessions. Depending on the deal, landlords may offer up to 6 months of free rent.
  • A plan for the furniture side, so the space works on move in day.

Why furniture belongs in the conversation

Most people budget for rent and forget the desks, chairs and conference room. We look at both at the same time. Once you pick a space, we plan the layout in 3D and furnish it, usually 2 to 4 weeks after you approve the plan.
