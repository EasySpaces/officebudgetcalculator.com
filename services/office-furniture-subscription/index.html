Office Furniture Subscription

An Easy Spaces subscription furnishes your office for a monthly fee instead of a large purchase. Plans start from $379/mo with $0 upfront, and delivery and install are built into your monthly price.

The shortest term is 12 months, so a one-year lease works. Longer terms lower the monthly price. At lease end, we remove the furniture at no charge.

Prefer to own the furniture over time? Rent-to-own starts from $349/mo with $0 upfront, with delivery & install quoted separately.
