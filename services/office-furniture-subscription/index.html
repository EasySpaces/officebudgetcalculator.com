Office Furniture Subscription

A subscription gets your team into a furnished office without a big check up front. You pay one monthly price, starting from $379/mo, and we handle the rest.

The minimum term is 12 months. Delivery and setup are blended into your monthly price, so there is no separate install bill to plan around.

Who it fits

  • Teams signing a shorter lease who do not want to own furniture they may outgrow.
  • Companies that want to keep cash for hiring and growth.
  • Anyone who wants one predictable monthly number.

How it works

  1. Book a free call and tell us about your space and headcount.
  2. We plan the layout in 3D so you can see it before anything is ordered.
  3. You approve the plan and the monthly price.
  4. Furniture arrives and is set up, usually 2 to 4 weeks after you approve.

Subscription or rent-to-own?

If you want to own the furniture, look at rent-to-own: $349/mo with $0 upfront, and the furniture is yours at the end. Delivery and install are not included with rent-to-own. If you would rather not own it, a subscription keeps things simple.
