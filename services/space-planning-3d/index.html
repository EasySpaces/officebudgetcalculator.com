Space Planning & 3D Renderings

Before you order a single desk, you should see how your office will work. Every Easy Spaces project starts with a free consultation and a 3D layout of your space.

You see where desks, private offices, the conference room and reception go. You catch problems on screen, not after delivery.

What we need from you

  • A floor plan, a listing or the suite address.
  • Your headcount today and where you expect it to be.
  • The rooms you need, like a conference room, break area or reception.

Why the layout matters

The layout sets your budget. More workstations, more cost. A good layout can help you fit your team in less space. Still choosing between spaces? We can lay out more than one so you can compare.

After the layout

Once you approve the plan, you choose how to pay. Lead time is usually 2 to 4 weeks.
