Furnished Lease Solutions for Landlords

Some tenants want a suite they can use on day one. A furnished suite gives them that. Easy Spaces works with landlords and property managers to furnish suites so they can be marketed and leased as move in ready.

How it works

  1. Book a free call and tell us about the suite.
  2. We plan it in 3D so you can use the layout in your marketing.
  3. You pick how to pay for the furniture.
  4. We deliver and install, usually 2 to 4 weeks after you approve.

Options for landlords

You can buy new at $20 per square foot max, and most projects come in under that. Used furniture typically runs about $12 per square foot. Limited inventory. Availability varies. Prefer to spread the cost? Rent-to-own is $349/mo with $0 upfront, delivery and install are quoted separately, and you own the furniture at the end. A subscription starts from $379/mo with $0 upfront and a 12-month minimum. Delivery and install are built into the monthly price, with free removal at the end of the term.
