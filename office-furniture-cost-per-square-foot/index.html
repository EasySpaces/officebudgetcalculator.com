Office Furniture Cost per Square Foot

Short answer: Easy Spaces plans office furniture to a $20 per sq ft max budget, and most projects come in under that. For a 2,000 sq ft office, that means a budget ceiling of $40,000 if you buy outright. You can also spread the cost monthly with $0 upfront.

Use the Office Budget Calculator to enter your square footage and compare buying, rent-to-own and subscription side by side.

How to Estimate Your Budget

Multiply your usable square footage by $20 to get a max budget. Example: 1,500 sq ft x $20 = $30,000 max. Your final number depends on headcount, layout and product choices, which we work out in a free consultation with a 3D layout.

Ways to Pay

  • Rent-to-own: from $349/mo with $0 upfront. Delivery & install are quoted separately.
  • Subscription: from $379/mo with $0 upfront. Delivery and install are built into your monthly price. The shortest term is 12 months, and longer terms lower the monthly price.
  • Buy outright: plan to a $20 per sq ft max budget. Most projects come in under that.
