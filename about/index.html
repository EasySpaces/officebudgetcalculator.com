About Easy Spaces

Easy Spaces helps Phoenix and Las Vegas businesses furnish their offices with rent-to-own, subscription or purchase options, and connects them with tenant representation when they need space.

Easy Spaces is led by Jason Bowman, President. Jason works with business owners as a workplace strategist and advisor, helping them plan an office that fits their team and budget. Easy Spaces has installed 26,923+ chairs and 1,833+ desks.
