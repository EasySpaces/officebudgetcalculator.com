Three ways to get the furniture

Tell us about your office and we will help you pick the path that fits.

Purchase

Buy new, commercial-grade furniture. Plan on $20 per sq ft at most. Most projects come in under that.

Rent-to-own

From $349/mo with $0 upfront. Delivery and installation are quoted separately. You own the furniture at the end.

Subscription

From $379/mo with $0 upfront and a 12-month minimum. Delivery and installation are built into the monthly price, and removal at the end of the term is at no charge.
